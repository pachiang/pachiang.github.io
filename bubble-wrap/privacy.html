Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026 · 繁體中文版

Pop Pause: Bubble Wrap ASMR (“啵啵”, “the app”) is made by an independent developer, Poan Chiang (“we”). This policy explains what information the app handles.

What we collect

We do not run servers for the app, do not ask you to create an account, and do not collect your name, email, contacts, location or photos. Your sheets, settings and progress are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

The optional Premium unlock is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. The app asks Apple only whether Premium has been purchased on your Apple Account.

Advertising

The free version can show a rewarded ad when you choose to watch one for extra sheets. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. The app requests non-personalized ads and does not ask for permission to track you (it does not access the Advertising Identifier, IDFA).

To deliver, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may process information such as your IP address, device and app information, approximate location derived from the IP address, and how you interact with the ad. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent message before ads are requested. Where required, you can change your choice at any time in Menu → Settings → Privacy options. Premium users are never shown ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Questions: rainbow1994@gmail.com.

隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 24 日

「Pop Pause: Bubble Wrap ASMR（啵啵）」（以下稱本 App）由獨立開發者 Poan Chiang（以下稱我們）製作。本政策說明本 App 處理哪些資訊。

我們收集的資料

本 App 沒有自己的伺服器，不需要註冊帳號，也不收集你的姓名、電子郵件、聯絡人、位置或照片。泡泡紙、設定與進度只儲存在你的裝置上，刪除 App 時一併刪除。

購買

可選購的 Premium 永久解鎖透過 Apple App Store 販售，付款由 Apple 處理，我們看不到你的付款資料。本 App 只會向 Apple 查詢你的 Apple 帳號是否已購買 Premium。

廣告

免費版在你主動選擇「看一段廣告」換取張數時播放獎勵廣告，廣告由 Google AdMob 提供。本 App 只請求非個人化廣告，也不會要求追蹤權限（不存取廣告識別碼 IDFA）。

為了投放、限制頻率、成效衡量及防範詐欺，Google 可能處理你的 IP 位址、裝置與 App 資訊、由 IP 推估的大致位置，以及你與廣告的互動。詳見 Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資訊。

在歐洲經濟區、英國及瑞士，本 App 會先透過 Google 的同意訊息取得你的同意才請求廣告；法規要求時，可隨時在「選單 → 設定 → 隱私權選項」更改選擇。Premium 使用者不會看到廣告。

兒童

本 App 並非以 13 歲以下兒童為對象，我們不會蓄意收集兒童的個人資料。

變更與聯絡

本政策如有變更，會更新此頁面與生效日期。如有疑問請來信：rainbow1994@gmail.com。
